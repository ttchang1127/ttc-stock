Item 7.01.
Regulation FD Disclosure.
Press Release
The joint press release furnished as Exhibit 99.1 hereto also announced that Marvell and Lumentum will be hosting a joint investor conference call at 8:00 a.m., Eastern Time, on November 2, 2026, to discuss the transactions contemplated by the Merger Agreement. The presentation materials for the conference call and webcast are attached hereto as Exhibit 99.2.
The information in Item 7.01 of this Current Report, including the accompanying Exhibits 99.1 and 99.2, are being furnished and shall not be deemed “filed” for the purposes of Section 18 of the Exchange Act, or otherwise subject to the liabilities of Section 18. The information in Item 7.01 of this Current Report shall not be incorporated by reference into any registration statement or other document filed pursuant to the Securities Act or the Exchange Act, regardless of any general incorporation language contained in such filing.
Additional Information About the Transaction and Where to Find It
This communication relates to a proposed transaction between Marvell and Lumentum (the “Transaction”). In connection with the Transaction, Marvell intends to file with the SEC a registration statement on Form S-4 (the “Form S-4”) that will include a joint proxy statement for Marvell’s and Lumentum’s respective stockholders and that will also constitute a prospectus with respect to the shares of Marvell’s common stock to be issued in the Transaction (the “Joint Proxy Statement/Prospectus”). The definitive Joint Proxy Statement/Prospectus (if and when available) will be mailed to stockholders of Marvell and Lumentum after the Form S-4 is declared effective by the SEC. This communication is not a substitute for the Joint Proxy Statement/Prospectus or registration statement. INVESTORS AND SECURITY HOLDERS ARE URGED TO READ THE FORM S-4, JOINT PROXY STATEMENT/PROSPECTUS AND ANY OTHER RELEVANT DOCUMENTS THAT MAY BE FILED WITH THE SEC IN CONNECTION WITH THE TRANSACTION OR INCORPORATED BY REFERENCE INTO THE FORM S-4 AND THE JOINT PROXY STATEMENT/PROSPECTUS (AS WELL AS ANY AMENDMENTS OR SUPPLEMENTS THERETO), CAREFULLY AND IN THEIR ENTIRETY WHEN THEY BECOME AVAILABLE BECAUSE THEY WILL CONTAIN IMPORTANT INFORMATION ABOUT MARVELL,
LUMENTUM, THE TRANSACTION AND RELATED MATTERS. Investors and security holders will be able to obtain free copies of the Form S-4 and Joint Proxy Statement/Prospectus and other documents containing important information about Marvell, Lumentum and the Transaction, once such documents are filed with the SEC through the website maintained by the SEC at https://www.sec.gov . Copies of the documents filed with the SEC by Marvell will be available online free of charge on Marvell’s website at https://investor.marvell.com or by contacting Marvell’s Investor Relations department at ir@marvell.com . Copies of the documents filed with the SEC by Lumentum will be available online free of charge on Lumentum’s website at https://investor.lumentum.com or by contacting Lumentum’s Investor Relations department at investor.relations@lumentum.com .
Participants in the Merger Solicitation
Marvell, Lumentum and certain of their respective directors and executive officers may be deemed to be participants in the solicitation of proxies in respect of the Transaction. Information about the directors and executive officers of Marvell, including a description of their direct or indirect interests, by security holdings or otherwise, is set forth in Marvell’s proxy statement for its 2026 Annual Meeting of Stockholders, which was filed with the SEC on May 13, 2026 and is available online at https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001835632/000110465926060253/tm261486-1_def14a.htm , under the headings “Proposal No. 1 Election of Directors”, “Executive Officers of the Company”, “Security Ownership of Certain Beneficial Owners and Management”, “Executive Compensation” and “Certain Relationships and Related Party Transactions” and Marvell’s Annual Report on Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended January 31, 2026, which was filed with the SEC on March 11, 2026 and is available online at https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001835632/000183563226000011/mrvl-20260131.htm , under the heading “Security Ownership of Certain Beneficial Owners and Management and Related Stockholder Matters”. To the extent holdings of Marvell’s securities by its directors or executive officers have changed since the amounts set forth in Marvell’s definitive proxy statement for its 2026 Annual Meeting of Stockholders, such changes have been or will be reflected on Initial Statement of Beneficial Ownership of Securities on Form 3, Statement of Changes in Beneficial Ownership on Form 4 or Annual Statement of Changes in Beneficial Ownership on Form 5 filed with the SEC, which are available online at https://www.sec.gov/edgar/browse/?CIK=0001835632&owner=exclude . To the extent holdings of Lumentum’s securities by its directors or executive officers have changed since the amounts set forth in Lumentum’s definitive proxy statement for its 2026 Annual Meeting of Stockholders, such changes have been or will be reflected on Initial Statement of Beneficial Ownership of Securities on Form 3, Statement of Changes in Beneficial Ownership on Form 4, or Annual Statement of Changes in Beneficial Ownership on Form 5 filed with the SEC, which are available online at https://www.sec.gov/edgar/browse/?CIK=0001633978&owner=exclude . Other information regarding the participants in the proxy solicitations and a description of their direct and indirect interests, by security holdings or otherwise, will be contained in the Joint Proxy Statement/Prospectus and other relevant materials to be filed with the SEC regarding the Transaction when such materials become available.
