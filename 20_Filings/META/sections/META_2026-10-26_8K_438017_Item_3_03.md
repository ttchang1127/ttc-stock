Item 3.03. Material Modification to Rights of Security Holders.
On October 28, 2026, the Company filed the Certificates of Designations with the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware to establish the preferences, limitations, and relative rights of the Preferred Stock. The Original Certificate of Designations became effective upon filing, and the Restated Charter Certificate of Designations has an effective date of January 19, 2027.
Subject to certain exceptions, under the terms of the Preferred Stock, so long as any share of Preferred Stock remains outstanding, no dividend or distribution may be declared or paid on the Class A Common Stock, the Class C Common Stock (if issued) or any other shares of junior stock, and no Class A Common Stock, Class C Common Stock (if issued) or other junior stock or parity stock may be, directly or indirectly, purchased, redeemed, or otherwise acquired for consideration by the Company or any of its subsidiaries unless all accumulated and unpaid dividends for all preceding dividend periods have been declared and paid upon, or a sufficient sum or number of shares of the Class A Common Stock or Class C Common Stock, as the case may be, has been set apart for the payment of such dividends upon, all outstanding shares of Preferred Stock.
Holders of the Depositary Shares will be entitled to a proportional fractional interest in the rights and preferences of the Preferred Stock, including conversion, dividend, liquidation, and voting rights, subject to the provisions of the Deposit Agreement. The Preferred Stock will accumulate dividends (which may be paid in cash or, subject to certain limitations, in shares of the Class A Common Stock or in any combination of cash and shares of the Class A Common Stock) at a rate per annum equal to 6.50% on the liquidation preference thereof. The liquidation preference of the Preferred Stock is $1,000 per share. Dividends on the Preferred Stock will be payable when, as and if declared
by the Company’s board of directors (or an authorized committee thereof), on February 15, May 15, August 15, and November 15 of each year, beginning on February 15, 2027 and ending on, and including, August 15, 2029. Unless earlier converted, each outstanding share of Preferred Stock will automatically convert for settlement on or about August 15, 2029, into between 1.3620 and 1.6000 shares of Class A Common Stock (and, correspondingly, each Depositary Share will automatically convert into between 0.0681 and 0.0800 shares of Class A Common Stock), subject to customary anti-dilution adjustments, determined based on the average volume-weighted average price of the Class A Common Stock over the 20 consecutive trading day period beginning on, and including, the 21st scheduled trading day prior to August 15, 2029 (the “final averaging period”). Notwithstanding the foregoing, upon the issuance of the Class C Common Stock and satisfaction of certain other conditions, (1) unless earlier converted, each share of Preferred Stock (and, correspondingly, each Depositary Share) will be convertible into shares of Class C Common Stock for settlement on or about August 15, 2029 in a number not less than the then-current minimum conversion rate nor greater than the then-current maximum conversion rate, determined based on the average volume-weighted average price of the Class C Common Stock during the final averaging period, (2) other than during a fundamental change conversion period (as defined in the Certificates of Designations), at any time after the 50th trading day immediately following the Class C Dividend Trigger Date (as defined in the Certificates of Designations) and prior to the mandatory conversion settlement date, a holder of 20 Depositary Shares may cause the Depositary to convert one share of Preferred Stock, on such holder’s behalf, into a number of shares of Class C Common Stock equal to the then-current minimum conversion rate and (3) cumulative dividends with respect to the Preferred Stock may be paid in cash or, subject to certain limitations, in shares of Class C Common Stock or in any combination of cash and Class C Common Stock, at the election of the Company. If a holder of 20 Depositary Shares causes the Depositary to convert one share of Preferred Stock, on such holder’s behalf, during a specified period beginning on the fundamental change reference date with respect to a fundamental change, the conversion rate will be adjusted under certain circumstances, and such holder will also be entitled to a make-whole dividend amount.
In addition, in the event of the Company’s voluntary or involuntary liquidation, winding-up or dissolution, each holder of Preferred Stock will be entitled to receive an amount per share of Preferred Stock equal to the greater of (x) $1,000 per share of the Preferred Stock plus an amount equal to any accumulated and unpaid dividends per share of such Preferred Stock to, but excluding, the date fixed for such liquidation, winding-up or dissolution and (y)(i) the per share amount to be paid or distributed to holders of the Class A Common Stock or Class C Common Stock, as applicable, then underlying the Preferred Stock in connection with such voluntary or involuntary liquidation, winding-up or dissolution, multiplied by (ii) the then-current maximum conversion rate of the Preferred Stock, to be paid out of the Company’s assets available for distribution to the Company’s shareholders, after satisfaction of liabilities to the Company’s creditors and holders of any senior stock and before any payment or distribution is made to holders of junior stock, including the Class A Common Stock and, if issued, the Class C Common Stock.
